# Fictional Vendor Due Diligence — v1.0

## Vendor screening
Check the vendor identifier against the synthetic vendor registry. Unknown vendors and vendors whose status is review_required must be referred to a human reviewer. A flagged vendor is not evidence of fraud.

## Privacy and evidence
Do not enter personal, confidential or real investigation data. Policy answers must cite supplied evidence. If the supplied evidence does not answer the question, state insufficient evidence and escalate.
