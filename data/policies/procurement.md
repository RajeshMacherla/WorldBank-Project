# Fictional Procurement Policy — v1.0

## Competitive bidding
Procurements of USD 10000 or more require documented competitive bidding justification. A missing justification must be escalated to a human procurement reviewer. This synthetic rule is for demonstration only.

## Senior approval
Procurements of USD 50000 or more require senior approval before proceeding. The assistant cannot grant approval or execute a procurement transaction.

## Record keeping
Retain the procurement purpose, amount, vendor identifier, bidding justification and human review outcome in the case record. Only public synthetic documents may be used in this demonstration.
